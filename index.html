What services do you offer?

Shopify and WordPress websites, WooCommerce stores, full stack web apps, mobile apps, GoHighLevel funnels, graphic design and UI/UX, video editing, SEO and speed optimisation, Excel and data work, and domain and hosting setup. One person for everything your business needs online.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. I work fully remotely, and my clients include businesses in the USA, Canada, Australia, Sweden, Hungary and Saudi Arabia.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A landing page is quick, a full store or web app takes longer. You get a clear timeline and quote before any work starts, and regular progress updates on WhatsApp.

Can you fix or redesign my existing website?

Yes. I fix bugs, speed up slow sites, improve mobile layouts, clean up plugins and redesign outdated websites without losing your content or SEO.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. I offer updates, content changes, troubleshooting, backups and ongoing maintenance, so your website keeps working long after launch.

Can you build a website for my business from scratch?

Yes. I design and develop complete business websites, online stores and landing pages from scratch, including domain, hosting, content setup, mobile design and basic SEO, so you get a website that is ready to bring in customers.

Are you a Shopify or WordPress developer?

Both. I build and customise Shopify stores and WordPress or WooCommerce websites every week, and I also work with Wix, GoHighLevel and custom code when a project needs it.
